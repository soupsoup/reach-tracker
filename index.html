How verification works

LinkedIn search buttons open public search-engine queries; automatic network-wide LinkedIn discovery is unavailable. Search hits alone do not establish a share. Saved post URLs are checked on demand and during scheduled collection. Verification requires the exact article URL in the requested post’s public structured data; unrelated post counters, login walls and shortened links remain unverified. Bluesky and Hacker News observations require an exact canonical article URL in a post’s link metadata. Search results are bounded to 100 per platform. Source pages are checked for direct backlinks; syndication requires editorial attribution evidence. Candidate sources are excluded from verified totals. Earlier verified source relationships remain in the ledger when a fresh check fails, with the original verification date and current failure shown.

Snapshots record raw public counters and collection status. Likes, reposts, comments and points are separate platform measures. They are not people reached. Follower counts and impressions are unavailable; sharers are ranked by observed interactions, with no claim about personal prominence. URLs submitted through the form are saved for twice-hourly GitHub collection, subject to scheduling delays. Share links open the exact URL without requiring a sign-in. New URLs show a live social check while their first scheduled snapshot is pending. Search engines and known source pages provide partial discovery; the collector does not crawl the entire web.
