How verification works

Bluesky and Hacker News observations require an exact canonical article URL in a post’s link metadata. Search results are bounded to 100 per platform. Source pages are checked for direct backlinks; syndication requires editorial attribution evidence. Candidate sources are excluded from verified totals. Earlier verified source relationships remain in the ledger when a fresh check fails, with the original verification date and current failure shown.

Snapshots record raw public counters and collection status. Likes, reposts, comments and points are separate platform measures. They are not people reached. Follower counts and impressions are unavailable; sharers are ranked by observed interactions, with no claim about personal prominence. GitHub collection runs twice hourly, subject to scheduling delays. Search engines and known source pages provide partial discovery; the collector does not crawl the entire web.
